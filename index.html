How to read thistap to fold

The brain is a standard left hemisphere. When you choose a gene, each region is coloured by how active that gene is there, compared with the gene's own average across the cortex: mint is higher, violet is lower. Hover any region to see its name and value.

Early regions (white dots): the entorhinal cortex and hippocampus, where Alzheimer's pathology usually begins (Braak stages I–II). The hippocampus sits inside the brain, so it shows as a glow. Comparison regions (hollow dots): five areas of the neocortex.

The verdict comes from six donated brains (Allen Human Brain Atlas). A gene is "higher" or "lower" only when at least 5 of 6 brains agree; otherwise it is "unclear".

The models were asked the same question three times each: once allowing "unknown", once forced to choose. Badges compare their answer with the brain data.

Brain data: Allen Human Brain Atlas (6 adult donors, left hemisphere), processed with abagen; regions from the Desikan-Killiany atlas. Risk genes: Bellenguez et al. 2022, Nature Genetics (GWAS Catalog GCST90027158). Model answers are recorded benchmark runs via Portkey, not live calls.

The question

Are Alzheimer's risk genes more active where the disease starts?

◎Disease starts here

Entorhinal cortex + hippocampus, then spreads to the neocortex [1]

≡71 risk genes

From the largest Alzheimer's genetics study [4]

⇄Ask AI vs measure

Model answers checked against 6 donor brains [2]

Why it matters: people ask chatbots health questions every day. No benchmark had tested this kind of question against real brain measurements [8, 10].

Related work

How this compares

GeneTuring [10]Found confident wrong answers on genomics questions.Here: mostly "unknown", but chance-level when pushed.
Med-PaLM / MultiMedQA [8]LLMs pass medical exam questions.Here: that didn't carry over to measured brain data.
Confidence studies [11, 12]LLMs tend to be overconfident.Here: confidence was flat, not useful.
GeneGPT [9]Database tools improve answers.Next: give models the atlas as a tool.
Brain atlas + Alzheimer's [5, 6, 7]Gene activity relates to where the disease spreads.Here: the same data, used to test AI.

Limitations

  • Only 6 donor brains
  • Hippocampus is one coarse region
  • Healthy brains, not diseased tissue
  • 2 models, 3 runs each
  • Mapped genes aren't always the causal gene

Next steps

  • Give models atlas lookup as a tool
  • Finer regions and single-cell data
  • Other diseases: Parkinson's, ALS
  • Famous vs obscure genes

What I'd do differently

  • Plan the forced-choice run from day one
  • Fix the gene source before downloading data
  • More runs and more models
